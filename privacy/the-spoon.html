Privacy Policy - The Spoon

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Developer: Andeel Studio

Contact: andeelstudio@gmail.com

The Spoon is a seated mixed-reality game for Meta Quest. This policy explains what information the game uses.

What the game stores

The game saves a small amount of progress and settings on your headset only:

  • stars and unlocked levels,
  • your choices (difficulty, seated mode, MR or VR, play-area size).

This data never leaves your device. We do not receive it, and we cannot see it.

What the game does not do

  • It has no accounts, no sign-in and no ads.
  • It does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, camera images, voice or purchases.
  • It does not send gameplay data to our servers. We do not run any servers for this game.
  • It does not use the room scan, spatial anchors or the camera images from passthrough. Passthrough is shown by the headset itself and the game cannot access it.

Hand tracking

The game plays with hand tracking (and also works with controllers). Hand and finger positions are processed on your headset by the Meta Quest system so you can pick up the spoon. The game uses them only while you play, does not store them, and does not send them anywhere.

Ownership check

When the game starts, it asks the Meta platform whether your Meta account owns the app. Only Meta handles this check. The game does not receive your name, email or any other account details, and we do not store the result.

Meta

The game runs on Meta Horizon OS and is distributed through the Meta Horizon Store. Meta may collect and use information about your use of the app (for example, diagnostics, crash and usage data) under its own policies, which are outside our control. See Meta's privacy policy: https://www.meta.com/legal/privacy-policy/ and the Meta Quest supplemental terms.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children. Meta's own age requirements and parental controls for Quest apply.

Deleting your data

All game data is on your headset. To delete it, uninstall The Spoon, or clear its data in the headset's storage settings. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete. If you have any privacy request, write to andeelstudio@gmail.com and we will answer it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and the new version will be shown at the same address.
